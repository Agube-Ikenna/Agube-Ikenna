# Sources, measurement scope and reuse

- **Dynamic World V1** — Google/WRI, CC BY 4.0. [Catalog and terms](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GOOGLE_DYNAMICWORLD_V1). Tree classification uses mean probability over July–October at each endpoint. This custom temporal threshold is not a calibrated probability of correct change. At least two source predictions are required per endpoint; prediction counts may include overlapping acquisitions. The collection starts 27 June 2015, and coverage is uneven. Processed 10 m exports were generated before the current repository build.
- **Hansen GFC 2025 v1.13** — Hansen/UMD/Google/USGS/NASA, [catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/UMD_hansen_global_forest_change_2025_v1_13), [official download and user notes](https://storage.googleapis.com/earthenginepartners-hansen/GFC-2025-v1.13/download.html). Canopy closure ≥30% in 2000, vegetation taller than 5 m; mapped land only. Annual loss codes 15–25 cover eleven calendar years. The historic gain band covers 2000–2012, so it is not used for contemporary gain. Gross loss is a stand-replacement disturbance, not necessarily permanent deforestation. Sensor/algorithm changes affect comparability. Raw tiles are retrieved from their official host, not committed here (~1.4 GB).
- **Landsat 8 Collection 2 Tier 1 Level 2** — USGS. [Catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/LANDSAT_LC08_C02_T1_L2). Identically scaled, QA-masked RGB composites supply visual context; they are not the area estimator. White gaps are masked pixels, grey marks insufficient paired Dynamic World observations.
- **FAO GAUL 2015 level 1** — [catalog and separate noncommercial terms](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/FAO_GAUL_2015_level1). Fixed boundary vintage for comparability; deprecated in Earth Engine. Respect FAO's data license and attribution. Source data and derivatives are not relicensed as unrestricted software. 36 states plus FCT; FCT is grouped with North Central. Nassarawa is normalized to Nasarawa in the zone dictionary.

Derived estimates retain source scope and limitations. Geographic-area normalization controls zone/state size only, not baseline tree cover. There is no independent reference sample, design-based accuracy correction, causal attribution or inferential hotspot test. The supplied sensitivity sample tests settings, not classification accuracy.

## Coordinate systems

`grid_metrics_*.geojson` is an **unaltered Earth Engine export** containing UTM metre coordinates but no CRS member. Its `analysis_crs` field identifies EPSG:32632. Do not open it as longitude/latitude. `grid_metrics_wgs84.geojson` is the corrected EPSG:4326 derivative for GIS use. The original contains 9,419 cells; 9,412 have positive mapped Nigeria area and enter the cleaned table and WGS84 derivative. Geographic boundaries are EPSG:4326; the dashboard map uses EPSG:32632 display coordinates, simplified for display only. Measurements use unsimplified boundaries.
